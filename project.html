Hold the Line

Game Design & Development

A shift-based survival game made for a game jam. You take paid gigs holding spots in lines — sneaker drops, concert tickets, court dates — and manage your comfort meters against the risk of losing your spot. Cutters, rain, cops moving the line, and clients demanding video proof all work against you. Hold it and tips roll in; lose it and the client never rebooks.
